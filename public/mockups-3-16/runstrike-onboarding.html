Design notes
③ Activity setup — live mileage preview
The mileage table updates as the player picks activity type and level, so the choice feels concrete before they commit. The note "you can override any individual run" is important — it defuses anxiety about getting it wrong. Defaults are stored but not precious.
⑤ Story intro — VN beats, not a transmission
Three tapped scenes replace the T-001 military transmission card. This is a personal story — Jane at 3am, doomscrolling, interrupted by a pet feeder alert. The pixel art placeholders mark where BG art and a character portrait (Jane, concerned) will sit. The story beat is domestic and recognizable before it becomes strange.
⑥ Story tab — clue cards, not transmissions
The story feed uses "clue" cards (orange border) rather than transmission cards (pink border). These are things Jane has noticed/collected, not messages from a mystery contact. The transmission format may appear later in the story when other characters start communicating through the network.
⑧ During run — story moment toast
When Jane arrives at Ben's apartment, a yellow "STORY MOMENT" toast surfaces the narrative beat (mess, open door, cat bolts). The run doesn't end — the cat chase extends it. The cassette tape is seeded in the neighborhood and visible in the HUD as something to come back for.
⑩ Post-run — companion first, stats second
The cat companion unlock leads the post-run screen. Ben's Headphones show as a story item with emotional framing (the fog lifting). The seeded cassette tape is noted as something to collect next run — creating immediate pull for Run 2. Mileage stats are there but not the headline.
