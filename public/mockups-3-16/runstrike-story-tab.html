Design notes
① At location
YOU ARE HERE card (cyan) anchors the tab — always first, always tells you where you are in the story world. Active mission below it with clear objective checklist. Side quests are visually separate (divider + purple accent). Locked quests show their unlock condition — players always know what's coming without being told what it is.
② Travel confirmation
The distance is the hero element — large, prominent, unambiguous. The warning box makes the commitment clear without being preachy. "What this means" bullets explain the mechanic without a tutorial. "Not yet" instead of "Cancel" keeps the tone low-pressure. This screen earns the weight of the decision.
③ Traveling
YOU ARE HERE becomes TRAVELING — same position, same cyan card, pulsing dot. Progress bar uses the same style as the weekly mileage bar on the Feed tab. Cancel is present but de-emphasized (small, red, below the fold). The active mission remains fully accessible — being on a journey doesn't block your main quest.
④ Just arrived
Arrival fires a new transmission (pink, NEW badge) — this is the "little something" when you complete a journey. It's in the same format as the existing story transmissions, so it fits naturally. YOU ARE HERE updates to the new location. The completed side quest immediately becomes actionable. Main mission de-emphasized while you're away from it — not blocked, just quieted.
