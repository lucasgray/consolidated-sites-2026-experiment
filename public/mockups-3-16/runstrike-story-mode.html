Design notes
① Story prompt — play now or later
Two equal choices — "Investigate Now" is primary only because the player is already in context. Both lead to the same place; saving for later just dismisses to the Story tab where the chapter stays available. Estimated time shown (10-15 min) respects player autonomy.
② ③ Mode indicators — colored dot badge
A persistent colored dot badge tells the player which mode they're in at all times: green = top-down, yellow = point-and-click. This handles the transition between modes without a full-screen cut. The badge sits just below the status bar and stays fixed while the game layer scrolls beneath it.
② Top-down: side alley is optional
The alley between buildings is clearly accessible but not required. The glowing ⬟ item hints at something collectible — entering the alley could reveal a passcode or clue that seeds a side quest. Players who explore are rewarded; players who go straight to Ben's door miss nothing critical. This pattern scales to the rest of the game world.
④ Cat dialog — "Not yet" not "No"
The second choice says "Not yet" rather than "No" or "Cancel" — it returns to the point-and-click scene so the player can examine other hotspots first. The cat can always be approached on a second tap. This makes the player feel like they chose a different order, not that they rejected the game.
⑤ Cat chase — pathfinding visible
The cat's escape route is shown as a dotted trail with direction arrows while it's happening — the player can see the cat making decisions (left wall → across room → door). This makes the cat feel like a character with agency, not a scripted object. Three approach attempts before escape is a reasonable middle ground between too easy and too frustrating.
⑦ Back to app — two-step quest visible
The quest in the Story tab shows both steps (Scout → Capture) but step 2 is locked/dimmed until step 1 is complete. The player immediately sees what's next. The cassette tape seeded on the first run now shows a distance and a tease ("Ben's voice?") — creating tension between the new quest and the old breadcrumb.
