Design notes
① Radar
The radar is the real-world view — your neighborhood, pickups, scouted routes. The RADAR | WORLD MAP toggle at the top of the tab is the boundary between the two map realities. Switching maps is an intentional act, not accidental.
② World map
Pixel-art terrain using flat color zones. Scrollable in both directions — the world is bigger than the screen. Fog of war on north, east, and south edges — you can sense there's more out there. A ???-labeled silhouette in the north is visible but unnamed, creating pull. Paths between locations are dotted (not yet traveled) or lit (traveled). Tap any location node to reveal its info sheet.
③ Traveling (Warehouse)
The orange arc around the location icon shows travel progress (35% complete). The path from Apt B shows a solid orange segment (distance traveled) fading to a dashed remainder. The TRAVELING badge above the icon keeps the state legible at a glance without opening the sheet.
④ Start travel (Downtown)
Downtown has a pink quest indicator (!) on its icon — there's something there, you can see it from here. Tapping reveals the quest and the travel CTA. This is where the story reveals the shape of the world before you commit to going anywhere.
