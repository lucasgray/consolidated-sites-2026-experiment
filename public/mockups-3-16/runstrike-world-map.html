Design notes
World Map · Subway diagram
Three named lines (A/B/C) with distinct colors. Transfer stations use a diamond ring to signal they're on multiple lines. Fog of war is heaviest at the edges — you can see the network has branches that disappear into unknown territory. The broken Line A spur east of Station Cross shows the world has history and damage. Unknown domes render as dim silhouettes with "? ? ?" — visible but unnamed. Pan the map to explore. Tap any dome to see its info sheet.
② Home dome (The Meridian)
Pulsing ring + YOU ARE HERE banner. Shows as a multi-line station (Line A dot + Line C dot). The player can see they're at the junction of two lines — one going to Station Cross, one going to The Hollows (where they're traveling).
③ Traveling to (The Hollows)
Progress arc around the dome icon (35% complete). The Line C path shows a solid traveled segment fading to dashed for the remaining distance. The sheet shows the progress bar and a "keep running" message — no CTA since you're already committed.
④ Start travel (New Caldwell)
Quest indicator (!) on the dome icon signals something is there. Sheet shows the distance, the active quest, and a Begin Journey CTA — but notes you'd need to travel via Station Cross first (18 mi total, two hops).
⑤ Offline dome (Eastern Ruins)
X through the dome icon, OFFLINE badge, greyed palette. The sheet explains it's unreachable for now — no CTA. Creates mystery: what happened here? Will it come back online?
